Signed 2024-07-29 Assented by Tinubu → Status In force 01 Background After 2-month strikes by NLC/TUC, the National Minimum Wage was raised from ₦30,000 to ₦70,000/month in July 2024. The Act also changed the review cycle from every 5 years to every 3 years going forward, and provided enforcement teeth: companies paying below the minimum face up to ₦500,000 fines per violation plus criminal liability for directors. 02 Key provisions · 6 § 01 ₦70,000/month national minimum wage (was ₦30,000) § 02 3-year review cycle (was 5 years) § 03 Up to ₦500K fine per violation § 04 Personal criminal liability for company directors § 05 Applies to public + private + LGA employers § 06 Federal Ministry of Labour to publish compliance reports annually 03 What changed in practice → First minimum wage increase since 2019 → Coincides with PIT-free threshold of ₦800K in NTA 2025 — minimum-wage earners essentially out of PIT net → Triggered most states to renegotiate their parallel wage structures 04 Who it affects Minimum-wage earners Employers State governments NLC/TUC Official source Federal Ministry of Labour Primary regulator / sponsoring institution ↗ Use this law on the site See PIT impact for minimum-wage earners Cross-linked tool that operationalises this Act 05 What it repealed / replaced National Minimum Wage Act 2019 More in Labour & Social Students Loans (Access to Higher Education) Act 2024 Amended version of the original 2023 Act — interest-free loans for undergrad/HND students at federal and state institutions, administered by NELFUND.
