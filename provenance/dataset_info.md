# Training datasets

All TaxSabi training data is generated by this team and verified against the
deterministic Decimal rules engine in `src/rules_engine/` (Nigeria Tax Act 2025
bands and reliefs). No third-party tax datasets were used.

| Dataset | Path in repo | Size | Source | License |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| DAPT corpus | `data/dapt_corpus/` | ~695K chars (~174K tokens) | Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and NTAA 2025 (cleaned public statutes), PenCom VPC Guidelines 2018 (public regulator publication), 6 original procedural documents written by the team | Government gazettes / public regulator publications; original prose by the team |
| DAPT replay | mixed at 15% (wikitext streaming) | ~105K chars | HuggingFace `wikitext` | CC BY-SA 3.0 |
| SFT v2 | `data/sft_v2/{train,val}.jsonl` | 1,929 examples (1,833 train / 96 val) | 490 engine-verified conversations + 1,250 single-turn Q&A generated by the team against engine-locked blueprints + 220 OASST1 general-chat examples | Team-generated content; OASST1 replay is Apache-2.0 |
| KTO v1 | `data/kto/{train,val}.jsonl` | 3,144 binary labels (2,984 train / 160 val) from 864 prompts | 552 engine-verified gold answers + 2,592 on-policy model samples labelled deterministically (engine totals, engine savings, required terms, citation register) | Team-generated |
| GRPO pool | `data/grpo/pool.jsonl`, `data/grpo/train_pool.jsonl` | 1,284 engine-grounded prompts (81 mid-tier used for training) | 550 KTO prompts + 529 verified SFT prompts + 205 fresh engine-locked scenarios | Team-generated |
| Rules engine | `src/rules_engine/` | 22+82+76 lines + `rules_ng_2026.json` | Implemented by the team from the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (Fourth Schedule s.58; s.30(2)(a)) | Team-authored code |

## Verification discipline

- Every naira figure in every assistant answer is engine-computed; a verifier
  (`scripts/verify_sft_generation.py`, copied to `provenance/scripts/`) rejects
  answers containing unsourced amounts or missing required terms/figures.
- The held-out probe (`data/eval/probe_prompts_heldout.jsonl`) is checked for
  zero overlap with all training data before every data change
  (`scripts/build_heldout_probe.py --verify-only`).
- Pidgin records were human-reviewed by the team during the SFT review pass.
